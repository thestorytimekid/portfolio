Maple.inc

Ads for an AI that answers the phone

Maple makes an AI that answers the phone for restaurants. Busy kitchens miss a lot of calls. Maple picks them up.

Other companies in this space all made the same kind of ad: a picture and a list of features. We tried something else. We used real calls between Maple's AI and real customers, and made them look like normal posts. People skip ads. Almost nobody skips listening in on someone else's phone call.

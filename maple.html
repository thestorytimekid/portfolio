Maple.inc

Maple makes an AI that answers the phone for restaurants. Busy kitchens miss a lot of calls. Maple picks them up. They needed ads people would actually stop for.

$63to win a customer, down from $300

What I did

  • Came up with the ideaEvery other company in this space ran the same ad: a picture and a list of features. I went with real phone calls instead.
  • Wrote the adsWe took real calls between Maple's AI and real customers, and I shaped each one into a short story people want to hear to the end.
  • Directed the adsI art directed how they look, so they feel like normal posts in your feed, not like ads.
  • Made them work as adsEach one still had to sell. A clear hook, a clear product and a clear reason to try it.

People skip ads. Almost nobody skips listening in on someone else's phone call.
